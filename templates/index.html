    02 Manifesto
    1. I.

      Interfaces should breathe.

      Every input is a small weather system. Cursors, cursored planets, cursored oceans — they should acknowledge you before your sentence ends.

    2. II.

      Colour is temperature.

      Blue and green are patience. Copper and orange are urgency. The palette is a feedback loop, not a decision.

    3. III.

      Motion is meaning.

      If a pixel moves without a reason, remove it. If a pixel refuses to move when it should, that is a bug, not a feature.
